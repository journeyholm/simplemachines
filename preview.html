How to play

🗺️ The Journey is the story: get the 500 lb boar 10 miles home. At each obstacle you read the scene, choose a machine (any of the 8, but not all of them make sense!), build and test it, learn the science, and add a page to your sketchbook. 🧪 Machine Lab is free play for training:

  1. Pick a machine on the left. There are six simple machines, plus two that put them to work: the Cart (wheels & axles beat friction) and the Raft Clamp (a screw that holds things tight).
  2. Build it from the materials pile: stones, logs, and vines. A glowing outline shows where each part goes.
  3. Drag the orange ✋ orb to push, pull, crank, or turn the machine.
  4. Drag the blue ↔ arrows (or use the sliders) to resize parts. The math updates instantly.
  5. If the hunters can't push hard enough, the load won't budge. Get more mechanical advantage!
  6. Check the Engineering Log to see the math, or choose I'll solve it and do it yourself.
  7. Press 📈 Graph to watch the math change live: the curve shows every size you could pick, the orange dot is your machine, and the green zone is "strong enough".
  8. Info boxes in the way? Drag their titles to move them (double-click a title to put it back), drag the corner to resize, or press ◧ to make them see-through.

Camera: drag empty ground to orbit all the way around, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to slide. Use the 🎥 Camera pad for preset views, Q/E to orbit, and 🔄 to spin 360°.
